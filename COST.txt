9. Limitations
total_bytes_processed is the byte count stored in run history. It is not a BigQuery
  billing export. Cached bytes, bytes written, and charges for a failed query are not in
  the supplied files.
Observed schedule dates that are not contiguous: 0 configurations. A daily rate uses the
  count of observed dates, not the calendar span from the first date to the last.
The hourly scenario and the writer and repeated-SQL scenarios state a replacement that
  was not run. Their dollar figures stay assumptions. The no-consumer figure is the
  observed rate only, and the usage extract can still have missed a reader.
Disabling a schedule is not deleting a table. This report does not select queries for
  deletion.
